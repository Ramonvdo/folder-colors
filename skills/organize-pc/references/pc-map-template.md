## Rules in force

- Depth: three levels inside an area at most.
- Files with a date: `YYYY-MM-DD_Type_Name_vN`; evergreen files keep a plain name.
- Downloads: no subfolders; Storage Sense deletes files untouched for {N} days.
- Desktop: empty at the end of the day.
- `00_INBOX`: holding pen, emptied weekly; two weeks old goes to `99_ARCHIVE`.
- Finished work goes to `99_ARCHIVE\{year}\`; nothing is deleted by the skill.
